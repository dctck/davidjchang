NFT Marketplace Offers

Turning a broad growth goal into a price-discovery system that helped buyers signal demand and sellers make better pricing decisions.

ROLE
Lead Product Designer
TEAM
COO, PM, Engineers, QA
TIMELINE
5 Weeks
IMPACT
+50% Transaction Volume

The Problem

Sellers and Buyers Were Too Far Apart on Price

The brief was intentionally broad: increase transaction volume on the marketplace. There was no feature attached to it, so I first needed to understand why people were not buying.

Marketplace activity showed plenty of listings but relatively few sales. Sellers were often listing assets above what buyers were willing to pay, while buyers had no way to signal a lower price. That created a clear gap between listed prices and real market demand.

Research & Discovery

How Other Marketplaces Made Demand Visible

OpenSea, Magic Eden, and OKX all used offers to expose buyer intent. The pattern suggested that negotiation was only part of the value. Visible offers also gave sellers a live signal for what the market would actually pay.

Make a Blockchain Limitation Understandable

Rejected or expired offers did not automatically return funds to the buyer. Instead of leaving that behavior hidden, I surfaced reclaimable funds through clear status labels, notifications, and a visible Claim action in the Offers & Bids hub.

That constraint shaped the system around three needs: market context before making an offer, one place to manage offer states, and an explicit recovery path when funds needed to be reclaimed.

Put the Decision Context Next to the Decision

Before entering an offer, buyers needed three numbers: their available balance, the floor price, and the best existing offer. I grouped those directly above the input so users could compare the market context before deciding what to enter.

When Opinions Differ, Test the Cheapest Version

I initially argued against counter-offers because they added another negotiation state. A stakeholder disagreed, so we shipped the low-cost version and watched how people used it. Post-launch behavior showed users preferred placing fresh offers, which gave the team evidence to simplify the experience.

Impact & Results

The Results

The feature increased marketplace activity, but the more interesting result was how visible offers changed price discovery.

Offers Became a Market Signal

Visible offers gave sellers a direct signal of buyer demand instead of forcing them to rely only on listing prices.

What I Learned

Use Real Behavior to Resolve Product Disagreement

When a disputed idea is inexpensive to build and easy to reverse, shipping a small version can create better evidence than continuing to debate it internally.

Market Context Can Teach Better Than Instructions

Showing real buyer intent gave sellers information they could act on without adding another educational layer.
