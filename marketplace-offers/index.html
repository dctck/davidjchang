NFT Marketplace Offers

Bridging the pricing gap between buyers and sellers to unlock marketplace potential and drive transaction volume growth.

ROLE

TEAM

TIMELINE
4 Weeks
IMPACT

The Problem

The Challenge

Our COO challenged us, "We need to increase marketplace transaction volume." That was it. No specs, no clear direction.

I started by digging into the data and talking to our PM. We had plenty of listings, but few sales. The disconnect wasn't obvious at first. Through behavioral analysis, I discovered sellers were pricing NFTs too high because they did not have any data avaialable to make informed decision. Meanwhile, buyers were interested but had no way to signal their intent.

Research & Discovery

Competitive Analysis

To understand what was missing, I conducted a competitive analysis of OpenSea, Magic Eden, and OKX. That's when it clicked: every major platform had an offers system. We didn't. Offers weren't just a feature, they were the missing piece that would create price discovery and market feedback. I also took note of what other features were complementing the Offer feature.

User Journey: Finding the Right Price

From Strategy to Execution

Once I validated that offers were the right solution, I worked closely with our CFO and engineers to understand technical constraints. Enjin's blockchain requires manual reclaiming of funds for unaccepted offers, a limitation that would alter the user flow.

I wrote detailed specs outlining three core flows:

  1. Item Detail View – Displaying all offers on an NFT with accept/decline actions
  2. Offers & Bids Hub – A dashboard in user profiles to manage all active offers
  3. Offer Settings – Letting sellers set minimum offer thresholds to reduce noise

Technical Considerations

Limitation

Early in the design process, I learned Enjin's blockchain requires buyers to manually reclaim unaccepted offers - funds don't automatically return.

UX Solution

Rather than seeing this as a blocker, I worked with engineers to design a notification system that would turn this into a feature, creating a "Claim" CTA in the Offers & Bids hub.

Solution Design

The Solution

We introduced a comprehensive offers system that would bridge the pricing gap between buyers and sellers, providing market signals and enabling price discovery.

Core Features

Visible Market Intent

All offers displayed prominently on NFT pages

Time-bound Offers

Expiration dates create urgency and clarity

Smart Notifications

Alerts for new offers, expirations, and acceptance

One-click Acceptance

Streamlined flow for sellers to accept offers

Impact & Results

The Results

The results exceeded our expectations. While we anticipated increased offer activity, the broader impact on marketplace dynamics was remarkable.

The Unexpected Win

Beyond offer acceptance, the feature improved overall price discovery, leading to better listing prices and increased buyer confidence across the entire marketplace.

Key Learnings

Network Effects

Increased activity from offers created positive momentum, encouraging more listings and buyer engagement.

Educating Sellers

The offers not only enabled negotiations but also educated the entire sellers about realistic pricing.
